CRUSH POEM

I got attached to you without planning it.
It just happened.
One day you were there,
and suddenly you mattered too much.
That's the truth I don't hide anymore..

THE WHATSAPP GROUP

I saw this girl in the DLS WhatsApp group,
And I don't believe in love at first sight.
But if I was on a lie detector,
My heart would float like a kite.
